Thank you for wanting to contribute.

This project is dual-licensed: openly under the AGPL-3.0, and commercially to
those who cannot accept the AGPL's obligations (see [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md)).
That second half only works if one party holds the rights to grant it. If parts
of the codebase were owned by different people under terms nobody controls
centrally, no commercial license could be issued for them — and the project
would quietly lose the ability to fund itself.

So contributions need a short agreement first. You keep your copyright; you are
granting rights, not giving them away.

## How to sign

Add a line to your pull request description:

```
I have read CLA.md and I agree to it.
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your@email>
```

That is the whole process. One line, once — it covers your future
contributions too.

## Questions

If any of this is a problem for your situation, open an issue or write to
<igor@horde.works> before investing time in a contribution. It is much easier to
sort out beforehand.
