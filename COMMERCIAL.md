## The open source license

By default this project is available under the **GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0** ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). Under the AGPL you may run, study, modify,
fork and redistribute the code freely, at no cost, forever. Personal use,
learning, research, hobby projects and non-profit use are all fully covered and
need no permission from anyone.

The AGPL asks one thing in return: if you distribute this software, **or run a
modified version of it as a network service**, you must release the complete
corresponding source code of your version — including the code you linked into
it — to its users under the AGPL as well.

That obligation is deliberate. It is what keeps the project open.

## When you need a commercial license instead

You need a separate commercial license from the copyright holder if you want to
do any of the following **without** releasing your own source code under the
AGPL:

- ship this software, or a derivative of it, inside a closed-source product
- operate it as a hosted or SaaS offering
- embed the engine, the game content or the map-authoring model in commercial work
- redistribute it under terms other than the AGPL

In short: if the AGPL works for you, use it and enjoy — nothing else is
required. If the AGPL does not work for your business model, that is exactly
the case a commercial license exists for.

## Terms

Commercial terms are negotiated per case. Both of these are on the table:

- **A commercial license** — a grant to use the software outside the AGPL's
  obligations, on agreed terms, typically involving a revenue share or a fee.
- **An outright acquisition** — full purchase of the project and its rights.

There is no public price list. Tell me what you want to build and at what
scale, and we will work out something that fits.
