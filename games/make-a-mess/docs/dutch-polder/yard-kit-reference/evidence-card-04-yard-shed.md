# Evidence card 04 — Zaan yard shed

The accepted `04-yard-shed-multi-angle.png` owns visible character. Historical
sources own the construction family; the yard-kit passport owns exact local
dimensions. Imagegen is not a geometry source.

## Source hierarchy

1. Yard-kit passport (`pasted-text.txt`): 4.60 × 6.40 m footprint, brick
   plinth 0.36 m, eave 2.45 m, ridge 4.15 m, frame and opening schedule,
   materials and 220-part ceiling.
2. [*Houten pakhuizen en schuren gebouwd in de Zaanstreek, 1600–1940*](https://www.zaans-industrieel-erfgoed.nl/pages_2/houten-pakhuizen.pdf),
   Vereniging tot Behoud van Monumenten van Bedrijf en Techniek Zaanstreek:
   timber yokes at 1.20–1.50 m in larger warehouses, straight knee braces,
   roof trusses on wall plates, purlins and boarded roofs; stepped wall boards
   25–30 mm thick, about 300 mm wide with about 50 mm overlap; brick piers and
   piled support under the historic large examples.
3. [Official Zaanse Schans history](https://www.zaanseschans.com/en/history/):
   Zaan timber frames use yokes, struts and corbels; exterior boards are fixed
   in an overlapping `getrapte weeg` assembly.
4. [Zaans Museum, *Dromen in hout*](https://zaansmuseum.nl/zien-doen/dromen-in-hout/):
   the regional wooden-frame building tradition and its relocation as complete
   buildings are part of the documented heritage.
5. Accepted multi-angle concept: tar-black vertical body, restrained white
   trim and doors, warm clay-tile roof, one six-pane side window, projecting
   hoist beam and hook.

## Authored type and axes

- Type: one-bay, single-storey yard/work shed with loft volume; not a house and
  not a multi-storey river warehouse.
- +Y up, +Z front/gabled loading face, origin at ground centre.
- Wall footprint: X 4.60 × Z 6.40 m.
- Roof envelope: X 5.10 × Z 6.90 m, including 0.25 m eaves/end overhangs.
- Fixed envelope: X 5.10 × Y 4.15 × Z 7.30 m including the 0.65 m hoist
  projection beyond the front wall.
- Human reference: cargo doors 2.00 × 2.10 m.

## Named geometry

- Ground → four-sided brick plinth ring, top y=0.36.
- Timber sill and floor → six wall posts (three transverse yokes) → ties,
  straight knee braces and longitudinal wall plates.
- Four rafter pairs 0.10 × 0.14 m → two purlins and ridge → boarded roof skin
  → overlapping warm tile courses.
- Wall cladding: vertical 0.20 m boards at exact 0.17 m pitch (0.03 m overlap),
  0.022 m thick. Cladding is absent inside the door and window openings.
- Front opening: two 1.00 × 2.10 m leaves on four 0.42 m forged strap hinges;
  leaves close a real opening and are not painted over a solid wall.
- Side opening: 0.90 × 1.05 m real opening, one physical glazing plane, 2 × 3
  white muntin grid.
- Hoist chain: internal beam tail → projecting 0.16 × 0.14 m timber beam →
  support brace → three forged links → hook. Projection from wall is 0.65 m.

The exact height/width values derive a 36.5° roof line to the wall plate rather
than a literal 40°. The exact envelope values own this conflict; the brief's
`~40°` is treated as descriptive.

## Rejection conditions

- Solid wall behind a door or window.
- Roof skin or tiles without rafter/purlin support.
- Hoist beam ending at the façade instead of continuing into the frame.
- Decorative hook without beam–brace–chain support path.
- Floating cladding, missing corner closure, flat painted tile texture, or
  generic cottage proportions.
- Gable, trim or roof envelope outside the named bounds.
