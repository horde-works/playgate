# Evidence card 01–03 — schouw, meerpalen, steiger

This card owns the canonical geometry. The accepted multi-angle studies own the
visible character; published sources and the object passport own construction,
dimensions and hidden topology.

## Source hierarchy

1. The task passport: exact dimensions, part schedule, water/bed datums and
   material family.
2. [SSRP, *Typebeschrijvingen Schouwen*](https://boekenplank.ssrp.nl/beschrijving-van-de-oude-scheepstypes/typebeschrijvingen-schouwen): the Frisian open schouw is a flat,
   shallow box boat with a mildly curved longitudinal line and sides that fall
   slightly outward; the class dimensions are 4.75 × 1.42 m.
3. Accepted PNGs `01`–`03`: silhouette, proportions, visible joints and the
   relative visual weight of timber and forged fittings.
4. Authored reconstruction: the 4.60 × 1.45 m local variant and its station
   interpolation. Authored values may not contradict items 1–3.

## Coordinates and datums

- Units: metres, +Y up, +Z front.
- Schouw: y=0 is the light-ship waterline; flat bottom y=-0.15.
- Meerpalen: y=0 is driven tip, waterline y=0.85, pile top y=1.60.
  The ring centre is y=1.40: 0.55 m above water, not above the driven tip.
- Steiger: y=0 is canal bed, waterline y=0.33, deck top y=0.63.

## Schouw construction

- Shared control lines: bottom edge, chine and sheer. Each line has seven
  longitudinal stations; side planks, gunwales, transoms and frames derive from
  those lines instead of independent boxes.
- Sheer endpoints z=±2.30; bottom endpoints z=±2.11. Their 0.19 m horizontal
  difference across the end-board rise produces the specified ~30° transom
  rake from vertical.
- Bottom: four 0.24 m boards, flat through z=±1.75 and rising 0.18 m to each
  end. No keel.
- Load path: bottom boards → five floor frames and side ribs → two side belts
  → gunwales; benches bear on the frames/sides. End boards close the hull.
- Rejection conditions: vertical transoms, pointed stem, constant-width box
  outline, detached frames, longitudinally parked oars, or any part below
  y=-0.15.
- Because the canonical zero is the waterline, general kit captures place their
  studio floor through the hull. The repeatable schouw object-study capture uses
  floor y=-0.25 and includes an underside view; absence of a visible floor in a
  general-kit image is not accepted as proof of a closed bottom.

## Meerpalen construction

- Two oak piles at 3.20 m centres, diameter 0.18 m, eight visible shaft facets.
- Each shaft is split at the waterline so later weathering can darken the wet
  zone without changing geometry.
- Four-faced 0.10 m cap; forged ring Ø0.14 m hangs from a separate bracket.
- Rejection conditions: flat top, fence-post reading, ring datum measured from
  the bed, or smooth plastic-looking shaft.

## Steiger construction

- Four driven piles support two longitudinal bearers; seven drained deck boards
  bear on them. No rail.
- Stair stringers are notched solids. Their horizontal ledges touch the exact
  underside of each tread; no diagonal member passes through a tread.
- Rejection conditions: floating deck, stair/tread volume overlap, rail, or
  missing 0.010 m drainage gaps.

## Fixed verification views

- Schouw: front/end, orthographic profile, high three-quarter, working interior.
- Meerpalen: front pair, profile, three-quarter, cap/ring detail.
- Steiger: stair front, profile, high three-quarter, deck drainage view.
