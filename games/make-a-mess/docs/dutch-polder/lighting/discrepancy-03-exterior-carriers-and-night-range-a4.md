# Discrepancy 03 — exterior carriers and night range, A4

## Audit findings

- H1 entrance lantern: its mounting plate was inside the right front-window
  bounds and visibly sat on the glass.
- H2 service lantern: its plate was centred inside the service-door clear
  opening rather than on the wall beside the jamb.
- M1 entrance lantern: the plate missed the outside edge of the right door post;
  the accepted oblique camera hid the gap.
- M2 eave lantern: its ceiling plate positively overlaps the front-eave member
  and was already correct.
- The 22 bulbs used a six-source nearby pool. With nine architectural groups,
  whole distant districts necessarily lost their real point-light cast during
  a cross-island view even though emissive bulb geometry remained.

## Corrections

- H1 is centred over the entrance on the solid door-head wall.
- H2 is beside the service door on a solid timber wall bay beyond the jamb.
- M1 is fastened through the actual right door post.
- Exterior plates now have an independent bounds audit: positive overlap with
  non-lighting opaque carrier geometry and no overlap with glazing, an opening
  or a dark door recess.
- Domestic/work/exterior lower contracts are now respectively `14 @ 16 m`,
  `22 @ 22 m` and `24 @ 24 m`.
- Every polder source reserves one representative from its `poolGroupId`; the
  twelve-source budget retains all nine current groups and uses three remaining
  slots for nearby detail. No billboard, beacon or fake reflected glow was
  added.

## Verification

- 4/4 exterior fixture types pass opaque-carrier/no-opening overlap checks.
- 22/22 bulbs retain physical clear-lens containment and real source ownership.
- Synthetic worst-order selection retains 9/9 compiled night groups inside the
  twelve-source pool.
- Fixed H1/H2 night detail cameras expose the plate, carrier and adjacent clear
  opening in one full-material view.
